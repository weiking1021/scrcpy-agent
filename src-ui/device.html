    What is set here is for this device alone and wins over the general settings. It applies to screens and windows opened afterwards.

    Stream

    Leave it empty to follow the general setting.

    0 keeps the device's own size. A smaller picture is lighter for the connection (1280 or 1920 are common).

    Apps

    The app gets a window of its own and the device's screen is left alone. If the device cannot do that, turn this off to open the app on the device's own screen.

    For devices that stop accepting touches in an app window once they lock or dim the screen (reported for some devices). The screen stays on for up to an hour while the window is open; the old setting is put back when it closes. Only one window of a device does this at a time.
